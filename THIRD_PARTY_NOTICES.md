# Third-Party Notices

## mpv

Swoop TV v0.2.7 does not bundle an mpv binary inside this source ZIP. On first Windows-native launch, the bootstrap downloads the pinned Windows x64 CI package for **mpv 0.41.0** from the official `mpv-player/mpv` GitHub release and verifies its SHA-256 hash before extraction.

mpv is a separate open-source project. Its licensing and source information are available from the mpv project and within its release materials. Swoop TV does not claim ownership of mpv, FFmpeg, libplacebo or their dependencies.

Source/project: `https://mpv.io/` and `https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv`
